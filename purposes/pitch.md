# Purpose: Pitch

**Goal**: Present the project in a compelling, persuasive manner.

---

## Description

The Pitch purpose reframes the project analysis as a persuasive narrative. Instead of a neutral assessment, the output should highlight strengths, emphasize value, and present the project in its best possible light. This does not mean being dishonest — it means choosing what to emphasize and how to frame it.

When the purpose is Pitch, the AI agent should prioritize impact, differentiation, and storytelling. The output should make the reader excited about the project. This is the purpose to use for portfolio descriptions, investor presentations, client proposals, and job applications.

---

## Behavioral Characteristics

- **Strengths-first**. Lead with what is impressive, not with what is lacking.
- **Outcome-oriented**. Describe results and capabilities, not just efforts and intentions.
- **Differentiation-focused**. Highlight what makes this project unique or superior.
- **Audience-calibrated**. A pitch to a recruiter emphasizes different things than a pitch to an investor.
- **Concise and punchy**. Every sentence should earn its place. Cut anything that does not add persuasive value.

---

## Output Modifications

- Open with the most compelling aspect of the project (the hook).
- Use confident, active language. Avoid hedging words like "appears to" or "seems to."
- Quantify achievements wherever possible — numbers are more persuasive than adjectives.
- Frame weaknesses as opportunities or learning experiences.
- Do not include a critical assessment section unless the audience specifically requests it.
- End with a forward-looking statement about the project's potential.
